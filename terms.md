# Family Automations SMS Terms & Conditions

**Effective date: August 25, 2026**

Family Automations is a private household notification service that sends informational SMS messages to family members who have agreed to receive them.

Messages may include school lunch menus, household reminders, schedule information, and other family logistics.

## Consent

Recipients are added only after agreeing to receive Family Automations SMS messages. Consent to receive messages is voluntary and is not a condition of any purchase.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and may include several messages per week.

Message and data rates may apply.

## Opt Out

Reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving SMS messages.

## Help

Reply **HELP** for help.

## Privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

See the Family Automations Privacy Policy for additional information.
