## Family Automations SMS Program

Family Automations is a private household notification program operated by Jared Merves.

The program sends informational SMS messages to members of a single household who have explicitly agreed to receive them.

Messages may include:

- School lunch menus and related school information
- Family schedule information
- Household reminders
- Family event reminders
- Other household logistics and informational notifications

Messages are not sent for marketing, advertising, lead generation, solicitation, or promotional purposes.

## Consent to Receive Messages

Recipients are added to the Family Automations SMS program only after explicitly agreeing to receive recurring SMS messages.

Consent may be provided verbally in person or by phone.

Before a recipient is added, Jared Merves asks:

“Would you like to receive recurring SMS messages from me with family information such as school lunch menus, schedules, and household reminders? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out or HELP for help.”

The recipient must verbally agree before their mobile number is added to the program.

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of any purchase.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on household needs and may include several messages per week.

## Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier and wireless plan.

## Opting Out

Recipients may stop receiving Family Automations SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP** to a message.

After opting out, the recipient will no longer receive recurring SMS messages from the program unless they subsequently provide consent to receive messages again.

## Help

Recipients may reply **HELP** to an SMS message for help.
