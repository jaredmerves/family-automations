# Jared Merves — Family Automations

Family Automations is a private household notification program operated by **Jared Merves, the registered Sole Proprietor**.

The program provides opted-in members of a single household with useful informational SMS notifications.

Messages may include school lunch menus, family schedules, household reminders, family event reminders, and other household logistics.

Family Automations is not used for marketing, advertising, lead generation, solicitation, or promotional messaging.

Participation is voluntary. Only recipients who have explicitly agreed to receive messages are included.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Recipients may reply **STOP** at any time to opt out or **HELP** for help.
