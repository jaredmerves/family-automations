# Jared Merves — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 25, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to the Family Automations household messaging program operated by Jared Merves, the registered Sole Proprietor.

## Family Automations

Family Automations is a private household notification program operated by Jared Merves.

The program sends informational SMS messages only to members of a single household who have explicitly agreed to receive them.

Messages may include school lunch menus, family schedule information, household reminders, family event reminders, and other household logistics and informational notifications.

Messages are not sent for marketing, advertising, lead generation, solicitation, or promotional purposes.

## Information Collected

Family Automations may collect and use limited information necessary to provide requested household notifications, including:

- Mobile phone numbers
- SMS messaging preferences
- SMS opt-in and consent information
- Information necessary to provide requested household reminders and notifications

## How Information Is Used

Information collected through the Family Automations program is used only to provide opted-in recipients with requested household SMS notifications and to operate the messaging program.

Mobile phone numbers are used only to deliver these notifications and manage recipient messaging preferences.

## Mobile Information and Data Sharing

Mobile information, including mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Personal information collected through the Family Automations program is not sold.

## SMS Consent

Recipients are added to the Family Automations SMS program only after explicitly agreeing to receive recurring SMS messages.

Consent may be provided verbally in person or by phone.

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of any purchase.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on household needs and may include several messages per week.

## Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier and wireless plan.

## Opting Out

Recipients may stop receiving Family Automations SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP** to a message.

## Help

Recipients may reply **HELP** to an SMS message for help.

## Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the limited personal information used by the Family Automations program.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

## Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Family Automations SMS program may be submitted by replying **HELP** to an SMS message from the program.
